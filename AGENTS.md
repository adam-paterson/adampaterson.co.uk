# Agent Design Direction

This repository follows the AP-6 portfolio design direction. Treat this file as the source of truth when making visual, copy, or component decisions.

## Desired Impression

The site should feel like it belongs to an experienced AI engineer who ships real systems and explains complex ideas clearly.

Visitors should leave thinking:

> This person understands complex systems and can explain them clearly.

## Design Philosophy

Communicate:

- Technical competence
- Thoughtful engineering
- Practical AI expertise
- Professional credibility
- Personal authenticity

Avoid anything that reads as a marketing agency, crypto startup, generic SaaS template, or sci-fi demo.

## Primary Aesthetic: Cyber Minimalism

Use Cyber Minimalism as the dominant visual language:

- Clean, calm layouts
- Generous whitespace
- Strong typography
- Monochrome foundations
- Hairline rules and precise spacing
- Subtle technical visual elements
- Data-inspired graphics and diagrams
- Low visual noise

The site should suggest advanced technology without looking futuristic or gimmicky.

## Secondary Influences

Corporate Grunge is allowed at low intensity, about 20 percent of the visual language:

- Light film grain
- Soft noise overlays
- Slightly distressed surfaces
- Technical sketches
- Editorial-style offsets

Do not use heavy grunge textures, punk aesthetics, messy composition, or distressed typography.

Brutalism is allowed at lower intensity, about 10 percent:

- Squared or lightly rounded surfaces
- Visible borders and rules
- Direct labels
- Strong contrast
- Exposed structure

Do not turn the site into a brutalist art project. Brutalism should sharpen the system, not dominate it.

## Implementation Rules

- Prefer neutral foundations with one or two restrained signal colors.
- Keep cards to 8px radius or less.
- Use grid, diagram, ledger, terminal, and system-map motifs sparingly.
- Use copy that is concrete and plainspoken.
- Use motion only where it clarifies state or adds quiet polish.
- Use texture as atmosphere, not decoration.
- Keep layouts scannable on mobile and desktop.

## Avoid

- Stars, nebulae, planets, orbitals, or space-exploration metaphors
- Purple/blue gradient SaaS styling
- Neon cyberpunk
- Crypto/startup gloss
- Oversized decorative cards
- Floating orbs, bokeh blobs, and abstract gradient meshes
- Generic "AI future" language
- Playful typo animations that reduce professional credibility
